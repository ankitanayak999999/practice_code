Before proceeding with the Core Card data mapping, we would like to understand the account hierarchy and data granularity maintained in the source system.

For reference, our existing Consumer Card (TC2) system provides data at two different levels:

- **Parent/Family Account Level:** One account can have multiple associated cards/cardholders. Financial attributes such as credit limit, outstanding balance, available credit, and other balances are maintained at this level.
- **Individual Card/Cardholder Level:** Contains individual cardholder information such as name, address, contact details, and other card-specific attributes.

Could you please clarify the following for Core Card?

1. Does Core Card follow a similar parent-child account structure, where multiple individual cards/accounts can be associated with one parent/billing account?
2. Or does each account operate independently, without any parent-child account relationship?
3. At what level are financial attributes such as credit limit, outstanding balance, available credit, and other balances maintained — parent account level, individual account level, or both?
4. If a parent-child relationship exists, what identifiers are available to establish the relationship between the parent account and individual accounts/cards?

This understanding will help us determine the appropriate granularity for populating our existing Credit Card Account Info, Credit Card Parent Account Info, and corresponding Balance views.
